Neural networks

A neural network is a small program that learns from examples — like studying flash cards. You show it two numbers, tell it the right answer, and it tries to guess. When it is wrong, it makes a small correction and tries again.

Use the controls below to train a tiny network yourself. Press Train and watch the predictions get closer to the correct answers. Tap any highlighted word for a plain-language definition.

Tip: start with the puzzle — it is a classic example that usually needs more than one to solve.
