Common questions

What is the context vector?

It is a blend of the four token meaning vectors, weighted by your attention. Higher weight on a token pulls the blend toward that token's numbers: the same idea as attention pooling, without learned keys and queries.

Why does focusing on “sat” prefer “quietly”?

In this toy, sat and quietly share a strong dimension (posture / manner). Dot product scores that overlap, so attention on the verb boosts the manner adverb.

Is this how production transformers work?

No. Real models learn attention from data across many heads and layers. Here you set the weights by hand so the ranking effect is visible in a few sliders.
