Common questions

How is this different from keyword search?

Keyword search looks for exact words. Semantic search matches meaning — a query about "scoring criteria" can rank a chunk about rubrics even when the word "rubric" is missing from your query.

What does cosine similarity measure?

Cosine similarity measures the angle between two vectors. A score of 1 means they point the same direction (very similar meaning); lower scores mean less overlap in meaning-space.

Why use top-k?

Models cannot read your entire document library at once. Top-k keeps only the best-matching chunks before passing them to the next step — retrieval narrows the field.

Why is my query dot red but far from a blue match?

This map squashes meaning into two dimensions for teaching. Real embedding spaces have hundreds of dimensions, so nearby matches can look farther apart here than they would in production.
