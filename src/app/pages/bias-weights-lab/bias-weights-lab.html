Common questions

What does the weight control?

Weight scales how strongly x pulls the score. A larger positive weight makes the sigmoid rise faster as x increases — a steeper decision slope.

What does the bias control?

Bias shifts the whole curve left or right. Negative bias pushes the flip point toward larger x; positive bias flips earlier. Together, w and b place the boundary.

Why is prediction 0.5 wrong for x = 0.5?

This lab treats “high” as strictly greater than 0.5. Sitting exactly on the sigmoid midpoint means the neuron has not committed to the positive class yet — nudge bias or weight until the score is positive at x = 0.5.
