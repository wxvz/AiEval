Common questions

Why is “Account locked?” a weaker first split here?

Locked tickets include both billing and access labels, so a single yes/no leaf cannot separate the table. Mentions refund groups every billing row on one side.

Would a perfect training score be enough to ship?

No. Four rows can be memorized. Real trees need validation and test checks so readable rules still generalize.
