Common questions

Why show the true mean in a teaching lab?

So you can check whether your locked policy matches the better arm. In production you would not see the true mean, only samples, which is why exploration matters.

Is locking a policy the end of learning?

For this lab, yes. Real systems often keep a little exploration, re-evaluate on held-out scenarios and revisit the reward definition when behavior looks wrong.
