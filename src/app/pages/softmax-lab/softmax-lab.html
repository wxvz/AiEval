Common questions

Why must probabilities sum to one?

Softmax models a single choice among options. The shares have to form a complete distribution.

Is this the same as the API temperature dial?

Related maths, different layer. Softmax lives inside probability conversion. Product temperature is an outside sampling control you will lock for fair evals later.
